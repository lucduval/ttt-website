# Your Personal Sign-Up Link

When someone signs up through **your** link, the form automatically credits you as their Brand Associate — no need for the client to remember your name or pick you from a list.

---

## Your link

```
https://ttt-tax.co.za/client-onboarding/?marketer=YOURNAME&service=SERVICE
```

- Replace **YOURNAME** with your first name, all in lowercase.
- Replace **SERVICE** with one of: `tax`, `insurance`, `accounting`, `advisory`.

When the client opens this link, they skip the "pick a service" screen and go **straight to the form for the service you chose**, with you locked in as their Brand Associate.

**Examples** (Sarah's links):

- Tax → `https://ttt-tax.co.za/client-onboarding/?marketer=sarah&service=tax`
- Insurance → `https://ttt-tax.co.za/client-onboarding/?marketer=sarah&service=insurance`
- Accounting → `https://ttt-tax.co.za/client-onboarding/?marketer=sarah&service=accounting`
- Financial Advisory → `https://ttt-tax.co.za/client-onboarding/?marketer=sarah&service=advisory`

> **Don't want to pick a service?** Just leave `&service=…` off. The client will see the service-picker screen but you'll still be credited.
>
> Use lowercase only. No capitals, no spaces, no full stop at the end.

---

## How to share your link

1. **Copy your link** (the one above with your name in it).
2. **Paste it** into a WhatsApp message, email, SMS, or social media post to your new lead or client.
3. **Send it.** That's it.

When they click it and fill in the form, you'll automatically be linked to them as their Brand Associate.

---

## How to know it worked

Ask your client to open the link. Near the top of the form they should see:

> **Your TTT Brand Associate**
> *(your name shown here, locked in)*
> Linked from your invitation.

If they see your name there, the link is working.

---

## Tips

- **Save your link** somewhere easy to grab — phone notes, email signature, WhatsApp "Message yourself."
- **Always use your own link.** Don't share someone else's, or you won't get credit.
- **Two Brand Associates with the same first name?** Speak to admin — you may need a slightly different link so credit goes to the right person.
- **Spelling matters.** `marketer=sara` is not the same as `marketer=sarah`. Double-check your link before sharing.
- **Service name must match exactly:** `tax`, `insurance`, `accounting`, or `advisory` (lowercase). Anything else falls back to the picker.
